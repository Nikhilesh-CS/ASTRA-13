Quantum Digital Signature (QDS) Security Testbed

How it works in plain words: Alice signs a digital message using entangled twin particles (Bell states). Because quantum particles cannot be copied without disturbing them, any attacker who intercepts or tampers with the message leaves an unmistakable physical trace (a jump in errors called QBER). Our system detects these attacks instantly using pure math and physics—no black-box AI required!

🎲

Bell State (Entanglement)

Simple Analogy: Magic Dice

Two particles linked across space. Whatever happens to one instantly reflects on the other, creating a private quantum bridge.

🚀

Quantum Teleportation

Simple Analogy: Blueprint Scanner

Scanning a quantum state, destroying the original, and reconstructing an exact copy at the destination using classical correction bits.

⚡

QBER (Error Rate)

Simple Analogy: Phone Static Alarm

Normally, fiber channels have < 3% error. If an eavesdropper tries to read the quantum bits, the error jumps to 25%+, sounding the alarm!
