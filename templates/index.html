Quantum Concepts Explained

🎲

Bell State (Entanglement)

Analogy: Twin Dice

Two particles linked across space. Whatever happens to one instantly reflects on the other, creating a private quantum bridge.

🚀

Quantum Teleportation

Analogy: State Reconstruction

Transmitting quantum information using pre-shared entanglement and classical Pauli correction bits without moving physical matter.

QBER (Quantum Bit Error Rate)

Analogy: Disturbance Alarm

Normally, channels exhibit minimal error (< 3%). If an attacker intercepts qubits in flight, errors spike past 20%, tripping the alarm.
